About NoteSwap

Good notes shouldn’t depend on who you know.

NoteSwap is one place where students share the notes that got them through a course, checked by moderators and organised by course.

Our story

In every university library before exams, students ask the same question: “Where can I find good notes?” NoteSwap started with a few of us in the middle of our finals, digging through blurry photos in group chats and outdated PDFs, while classmates down the hall had written brilliant notes with nowhere to share them.

So we asked: why should good study material depend on luck, or on how many WhatsApp and Messenger groups you’re in?

We taught ourselves web development between classes and part-time jobs, and tested NoteSwap at our own university. At first only a handful of people uploaded. There were bugs and complaints, but also the first moments where a second-year student opened a senior’s notes from another department, instantly and for free.

We kept listening. We added moderator checks so every note can be trusted, ratings so the best rise to the top, following so you hear when your favourite provider posts, and NoteSolve for the problems you’re stuck on.

NoteSwap is still growing, but the idea hasn’t changed: built by students, for students, to make studying easier and shared.
