How it works

Three steps from stuck to solved.

    Pick a provider

    Providers from your university and department are suggested first, with their solution ratings.

{# ---------- FAQ ---------- #}
Questions

Good to know

How do I pay?{% icon "chev-down" size="sm" %}

Choose a plan and confirm your order. An admin checks the payment and switches Premium on, usually within a day.

What happens when my Premium ends?{% icon "chev-down" size="sm" %}

You can’t send new questions, but questions you already sent still get answered, and you can read and rate every answer in NoteSolve. Buying a plan before it ends adds the new months on top, so you never lose paid days.

What if a provider can’t answer?{% icon "chev-down" size="sm" %}

They tell you why, and you can send the same question to another provider with one click.

Do I need Premium to read notes?{% icon "chev-down" size="sm" %}

No. Reading, downloading, rating and following are free for everyone with an account. Premium is only for NoteSolve.
